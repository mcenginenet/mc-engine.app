# Skin Repair & Photo to Skin

**Open it:** https://mc-engine.app/repair

**Skin Repair** fixes skins that look broken in Minecraft — old 64×32 skins, faces in the wrong place, missing arms or a see-through body — by showing every face of every body part so you can move, rotate, flip or redraw it. **Photo to Skin** (a tab on the same page) turns a photo or picture into a pixel-art skin; the photo never leaves your device.

**Quick start:** open Repair → your current skin loads (or drop a PNG) → click a face on the picture side to select it → fix it in the faces panel → **Open in Editor** or download it.

## How to fix a Minecraft skin: broken, old, or not showing in the game

Place each face of a picture onto the right part of the skin, one face at a time — perfect for wrong, stretched or old 64×32 skins — plus what to check when a skin looks wrong or does not show in Minecraft.

*On the website, with screenshots: https://mc-engine.app/docs/repair*

### Step 1 · Load the picture

Open **Repair** and press **Upload image**. The picture shows on the left. On the right you see every body part and its faces.

1. **Upload image**.
2. **Faces**: Head, Body, Right Arm, Left Arm, Right Leg, Left Leg — 6 faces each, 36 in total.

### Step 2 · Frame one face

Pick a face, for example **Head → Front**. Then drag the **orange box** over that part of your picture and drag its corners to resize it.

Use the **arrow buttons** to nudge it one pixel at a time. **Locked** keeps the right shape for that face; **Free** lets you draw any box.

1. The **orange box**: what goes on the chosen face.
2. **Adjust**: arrows, Locked / Free, Fit / Stretch, Live link.

> 💡 **Fit** keeps the picture's shape; **Stretch** fills the face exactly. **Live link** sends every confirmed face to the editor straight away.

### Step 3 · Confirm and go to the next face

Press **Confirm & Next** to save the face and jump to the next one. Repeat for every face you need.

When you are done, press **Complete & Save**. **Skip** takes what you have so far to the editor.

1. **Confirm · Confirm & Next · Complete & Save · Skip**.

### Step 4 · Skin not showing or looks wrong in Minecraft? Check these first

Many "broken" skins are fine — the game just reads them differently. Check these before you repair anything:

1. **Gaps or black stripes on the arms**: the arm size does not match. A skin painted for **Alex (Slim, 3 px arms)** needs **Slim** in the launcher; one painted for **Steve (Classic, 4 px arms)** needs **Classic**. Pick the same model you painted on.
2. **Black patches**: see-through pixels on the **base layer** show black in the game. Paint every base pixel; only the overlay (hat, jacket, sleeves, pants) may be see-through.
3. **Hat, jacket or sleeves missing**: in Minecraft Java open **Options → Skin Customization** and turn those parts on.
4. **The launcher will not take the file**: Java only accepts a **64×64** (or old 64×32) PNG. Export for **Java** — HD skins are saved at 64×64 for you. Bedrock takes a **.mcpack** up to 128×128.
5. **Still Steve or Alex in the game**: restart Minecraft after changing the skin, and check that you changed it on the account you play with. Some servers show only default skins.
6. **Old 64×32 skin**: open it in the editor or Repair — it is moved into the current 64×64 layout, with the left arm and leg copied from the right.

> 💡 If the picture itself is wrong — a face on the wrong part, a stretched image, a skin from another game — fix it face by face with the steps above.

## How to make a Minecraft skin from a photo or image

Turn one photo — or separate front, back, side and top photos — into a skin, with colour, pixel look and background removal. Nothing is uploaded.

*On the website, with screenshots: https://mc-engine.app/docs/photo-to-skin*

### Step 1 · Open Photo to Skin

Open **Repair** and click **Photo to Skin** at the top right. Your photos stay on your computer — nothing is uploaded.

Choose **One photo** (the front; the back is mirrored and the sides are guessed) or **Each side** (a separate photo for front, back, right, left and top).

1. **Photo**: One photo or Each side, then **Upload photo**.
2. **Photo area**: crop, rotate, flip and tilt your photo here.

### Step 2 · Set the look

**Model & size**: Classic or Slim, and 64, 128 or 256.

**Adjust colour**: brightness, contrast and saturation.

**Pixel look** uses fewer, flatter colours so the photo looks more like Minecraft. **Remove background** clears a plain background; **Fill empty pixels** fills the holes it leaves.

**Sides & back**: Stretch, Wrap 2, Wrap 4 or Empty, and **Mirror front to back**.

1. **Model & size**.
2. **Adjust colour**.
3. **Pixel look** and **Remove background**.
4. **Sides & back**.

### Step 3 · Save it

Press **Download PNG** to save the skin, or **Open in Editor** to keep painting. **Reset settings** starts over.

1. **Download PNG · Open in Editor**.
