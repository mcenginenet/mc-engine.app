# Your profile, favourites & notifications

**Open it:** your name in the top-right corner → **Profile** (needs a free account). Public profiles are at https://mc-engine.app/u/<username>.

Your profile is your page on MC-ENGINE: photo, banner, bio, social links, badges, your published skins and favourites, plus the security settings of your account. The account menu (your name, top right) has **My Skins**, **Profile**, **Upload skin**, **Favorites**, **Notifications**, **Settings** and **Log out**.

## Your profile: photo, banner, links, badges and security

Set your profile photo and banner, name, bio, pronouns and location, add social links, pin your best skins, choose who sees what, turn on 2FA and collect badges.

*On the website, with screenshots: https://mc-engine.app/docs/profile*

### Step 1 · Photo and banner

Open **Profile**. Click your round picture (**Change photo**) to upload a profile photo, and **Change banner** to upload a wide banner across the top.

Your profile also shows your stats, like public skins and followers.

### Step 2 · Edit profile

Press **Edit profile**. It has these sections:

**Profile**: Display Name, Username (3–20 letters, numbers or underscores — it tells you if it is free), bio, Pronouns, Gender (optional, private by default) and Location (optional — shown on your profile and used to suggest creators near you).

**Privacy**: **Profile Visibility** public or private — who can see what.

**Social links**: Instagram, Reddit, YouTube and Twitch. Type the username or paste your profile link. Visitors only see the icon and @name.

**Featured skins**: pin up to 3 of your public skins to the top of your profile.

**Security**: protect your account with **2FA** (an authenticator app code), and link more sign-ins (Google, Discord, Microsoft).

**Account**: your User ID, Public User ID, email status and the date you joined.

### Step 3 · Badges and sharing

Badges show on your profile: **Early Supporter** (joined in the early days), **Top Creator** (consistently great skins) and **Contest Winner** (won an event — see the **Events** guide).

On a public profile, **Copy profile link** and **Copy User ID** make sharing easy. **Orbit** follows the creator, and **More options** lets you report a profile.

The **Creators** tab suggests creators to orbit.


## Favourites, collections, notifications, reports and the weekly leaderboard

Find your favourite skins and collections, read your notifications, report a skin, comment or profile, and see who tops the weekly leaderboard.

*On the website, with screenshots: https://mc-engine.app/docs/favourites-notifications*

### Step 1 · Favourites and collections

Open your user menu (top right) → **Favorites**. The **Favorites** tab lists every skin you hearted; remove one with its heart.

The **Collections** tab holds your folders. Make one with a name and **Create**, then add skins with the **folder** button on any skin page (**Save to collection**).

Each collection can be **Private** (only you) or shown on your profile — click the lock / globe to switch. You can also rename or delete a collection, and remove skins from it.

### Step 2 · Notifications

The **bell** at the top shows new notifications; your user menu → **Notifications** opens the full list.

You hear about new followers, favourites, comments and remixes of your skins, contest wins and password changes. Click one to open it, or press **Mark all read**.

### Step 3 · Report something

See something wrong? Use **Report this skin** on a skin page, the report button on a comment, or **More options** on a profile.

Pick a **Reason** — Inappropriate content, Harassment or abuse, Impersonation, Spam, Stolen / plagiarized work or Other — add details if you like, and press **Submit Report**. The team reviews it.

### Step 4 · The weekly leaderboard

On **Home**, the **Leaderboard** has two tabs: **Weekly creators** and **Top skins**.

A creator's weekly score = downloads + 10 × new followers + 2 × rating stars received this week. It resets every Monday (UTC); early in the week you see last week's final board.


## Layouts, themes and fonts

Arrange the site your way (Classic, Unique, Darkness), choose a colour theme and switch between the Minecraft font and a normal font.

*On the website, with screenshots: https://mc-engine.app/docs/settings-look*

### Step 1 · Open Appearance

Open **Settings** (the gear icon) and click **Appearance**.

1. **Layout**: Classic (sidebar and top bar), Unique (floating assistant menu) or Darkness (pro 3D editor workspace).
2. **Theme**: Dark, Light, Pink, Sunset, Frost or Midnight.
3. **Font**: Minecraft (pixel font, like the game) or Normal (clean, easy to read).

> 💡 Darkness has its own colours, so the theme you pick is used in Classic and Unique.

### Step 2 · Other settings

**Account**: your sign-in and profile. **Saved canvas**: clear the skin saved in your browser (it is kept for 3 days so you can bring it back). **Shortcuts**: every keyboard key, and you can change them. **Feedback**: send us a message about a bug or idea.

**About**: the website version and a button to open this guide.

### Step 3 · Delete your account

Open **Settings → Danger zone** and press **Delete account**. Type your own username to confirm, then press **Permanently Delete My Account**.

This removes your account, profile and uploaded skins for good. It cannot be undone.
