# Skins gallery & skin pages

**Open it:** https://mc-engine.app/skins

The **Skins** page is the community gallery: skins people made and published, to browse, download, rate, comment on, save and **remix** in the editor. Every skin has its own page with a 3D preview.

## Every button — Skins page

- **For You** — a mix of new and popular skins, picked for you.
- **Trending** — the most downloaded skins right now.
- **Draw for me** — describe a skin and get help making it.
- **Upload Skin** — publish one of your skins to the gallery (needs a free account): pick a PNG, give it a name, keywords and a picture, choose **Only me** or **Public · Male / Female / Unisex**.
- **Skin cards** — each card shows the skin in 3D; **move the mouse over a card and the model spins all the way round**. Click a card to open the skin's page.
- **Previous / page numbers / Next** — more skins.
- **Creators to discover** (right) — creators to follow, with their skins and orbiters; **Next creators** shows more.

## Every button — a skin's page

- **3D preview** — drag to turn the skin; **Edit** opens it in the editor.
- **♥ Favourite** — save the skin to your favourites (the number is how many people did).
- **Add to collection** (folder icon) — put it in one of your collections.
- **Animated preview** (film icon) — a GIF or video of the skin walking and turning.
- **Share** — copies the skin's link.
- **Report** (flag, when logged in) — tell the moderators about a problem (stolen skin, bad content…).
- **Upload** (arrow icon) — opens the Upload / Export window to upload your own skin.
- **Rating, downloads, comments, upload date, favourites** — the skin's numbers.
- **Download PNG** — the skin file for Minecraft.
- **Remix in Editor** — opens a copy in the editor; when you publish it, it links back to the original ("Based on …", with **Compare**).
- **Rate this skin** — 1 to 5 stars.
- **How to wear it in Minecraft** — the three steps for the launcher.
- **About this skin** — the creator's description and tags.
- **Comments** — write a comment, like comments, sort by **Top** or **Newest**, **Show more comments**.
- **More skins: Similar / Trending** — related skins, with pages.

## Share skins with the community

Create an account, publish skins to the gallery, and download, rate, favourite, collect, comment on and remix other people's skins.

*On the website, with screenshots: https://mc-engine.app/docs/share-community*

### Step 1 · Make a free account

Press **Sign Up** at the top right. Fill in a player name, email and password, tick that you are 13 or older and accept the rules, then press **Create account**. Or use **Google** or **Discord**.

The skin you are working on stays where it is while you sign in.

1. **Log in / Sign up**.
2. **Player name, email, password**.
3. **Age and rules**.
4. **Create account**.
5. **Google / Discord**.

### Step 2 · Publish your skin

Press **Upload** in the top bar, choose **Public** in **2 · Export** and press **Publish to gallery** — or use **Upload Skin** on the Skins page. Give it a name and keywords so people can find it.

You can make a skin public or private, and edit or delete it any time from your **Profile**.

### Step 3 · Browse the gallery

Open **Skins**. **For You** and **Trending** show public skins. Use the search at the top to find a creator, a skin name or a skin ID.

1. **For You / Trending**.
2. **Upload Skin**.
3. A **skin card**: click to open it.
4. **Search**: creator, skin or skin ID.

### Step 4 · On a skin's page

**Download PNG** to wear it, or **Remix in editor** to change it (the original creator gets the credit).

Click the stars to **rate** it. The buttons at the top **favourite** it, **save it to a collection**, make an **animated GIF / video**, and **share / copy the link**. Logged-in users can leave **comments**.

1. **Favourite · Collection · Animated preview · Share**.
2. **Download PNG**.
3. **Remix in editor**.
4. **Rate this skin**.
5. **Comments**.

> 💡 Follow a creator with **Orbit** on their profile to see their new skins.

### Step 5 · Compare a remix with the original

A remixed skin shows "remix of …" with a link to the original. Press **Compare** to see both: drag the slider over the flat textures, or look at them side by side in 3D.

The same **Before / after** window is in the editor's top bar, where it compares your skin with how it looked when you opened the editor.


## Publish skins and manage My skins

Every option when you publish — name, keywords, who can see it, thumbnail, custom link, group — and how to edit, sort, hide or delete your skins later.

*On the website, with screenshots: https://mc-engine.app/docs/publish-manage*

### Step 1 · Fill in the upload window

Press **Upload Skin** on the Skins page (or **Upload** in the top bar → **Public** → **Publish to gallery**). Drop your skin PNG (64×64, 128×128 or 256×256), click to pick it, or paste it with **Ctrl + V**. Your skin shows in 3D — drag to turn it.

**Skin name**: if someone already used the name you can still use it. If this exact PNG is already in your skins, you are told so.

**Keywords** help people find it (ideas are shown), and **Description** is optional.

### Step 2 · Who can see it

**Only me** saves it to your profile only; you can publish it later from **My skins**.

**Public · Male**, **Public · Female** or **Public · Unisex** puts it in the gallery under that group.

### Step 3 · Thumbnail, custom link and group

**Thumbnail** sets how your skin shows up in the gallery, in 3 steps: **1 · Pose** (Standing, a preset, or pose it yourself), **2 · Style** (**Classic** front and back, **Single** full body, **Portrait** head close-up, **Turnaround** front / side / back, **Hero** low dramatic angle) and **3 · Direction** (Front, Front ¾, sides or Back). Pick a **Background** (White, Dark, Mint, Violet or any colour). **Reset to default** starts over.

**Custom link** (optional) gives your skin a nicer web address after **/skin/**.

**Group** (optional) sorts the skin into one of your own groups — pick one or make a new one.

### Step 4 · Manage My skins

Open **Profile → My skins** (or **My Skins** in your user menu). Filter by **All**, **Public** or **Private**, and **Sort by** Newest, Oldest, Most downloaded or Top rated.

Press **Edit** on a skin to change its **Name**, **Description**, **Keywords** and **Gender**, to **Make private** or **Publish to gallery**, or to delete it (**Yes, delete** — this is for good). The globe button opens its public page.


## Events: skin contests — enter, vote and win

Join themed skin contests: enter your skins, vote for your favourites, follow the live ranking and win the Contest Winner badge.

*On the website, with screenshots: https://mc-engine.app/docs/events*

### Step 1 · Find an event

Open **Events** in the menu. You see **Running now**, **Coming up** and **Past events**. Each event has a theme, its **Rules** and a **How it works** box.

Entries open when the event starts. Past events show the **Final ranking** and the **Winner**.

### Step 2 · Enter your skin

Press **Enter your skin**. Either **Upload a new skin** (it is saved, made public and entered at once) or **Pick one of mine** from your public skins. Each event shows how many skins you can enter (usually **3**).

Only public skins can be entered — event entries can't be "Only me". Changed your mind? **Take it out** removes the entry (its votes are lost; you can enter again while the event is open).

Share the event link so people vote for you.

### Step 3 · Vote and win

You get a few votes per event (usually **3** — the event's **How it works** box says how many). Press **Vote for this entry** on the ones you like; **Take your vote back** moves it while the event is open. You can't vote for yourself.

The **Live ranking** sorts entries by votes. When the event ends, the entry with the most votes wins the **Contest Winner** badge, and you get a notification.

> 💡 **Fair play**: only your own skins, made for the theme. Votes come from logged-in accounts with a confirmed email, and moderators remove entries that break the Terms or look like fake voting.
